# Printed business date corroborating a prefix-only ambiguity

Candidate work, not a release or annual acceptance. Version remains 9.6.8-rc.2.

## Evidence and repair

Two source-bound historical photos kept a stable suffix but the local English,
Chinese mobile and larger Chinese models repeatedly misread the prefix. Simply
replacing the engine or enlarging detection was not sufficient. Both photos
have a legible printed business heading and multiple unique complete fields
corroborated by the corresponding physical PDF page.

The new fallback permits a BODY-REVIEW CANDIDATE only with two strong original
code crops, a stable suffix, only a one-substitution prefix discrepancy, and
fresh, source-bound Chinese-model readings of the same original crops. Contrary
suffixes, incomplete/error observations, multiple regions, stale evidence and
independently confirmed opposite codes remain vetoes.

Final adjudication additionally requires:

- Two positioned original-photo views of the SAME complete business-date header,
  each at least 0.85 confidence, with consistent physical region and dimensions.
- The exact complete header in independently extracted PDF text and the same
  date digits / lamp context in two visible PDF readings. Only the final non-date
  Han glyph of the PDF visual heading is irrelevant to date identity; photo
  headings and PDF text extraction remain exact. No date digit is corrected.
- Calendar-valid full year/month/day, not a date from a folder, UI, clock,
  filename or a birthday without the business label. The namespace derived from
  the OBSERVED month must agree with the current code namespace.
- Two distinct paired whole fields unique over the COMPLETE current PDF corpus,
  with all existing contrary-body protections. No geometry-only or short-name
  fallback for this route, and no selection from missing PDF slots.

Raw contradictory words remain in the audit. The proof explicitly records
`fullCodeObserved=false` so a prefix reconstructed from independent header
evidence is not falsely described as a literal full-code OCR result. Existing
strict code / prefix-model paths retain their prior behavior. Online exact-ID,
upload and completion gates remain separate.

## Cache-version repair

During this work a newly added policy file was initially omitted from the old
handwritten fingerprint list. Two provisional diagnostics consequently had the
same legacy fingerprint despite a policy edit. Those runs are not frozen-version
acceptance evidence. A regression reproduced the omission before the fix.

Recognition identity now hashes all source code/rule files (including new nested
modules), retains mandatory core-file checks, sorts deterministically, and rejects
linked source entries. New or same-size edited modules automatically invalidate
cached recognition. This conservatively also invalidates caches for other source
changes; it never alters NAS originals. A final targeted run was repeated after
this repair with fingerprint:
`83487069c091e8763d51a157752dea8282368b84bc98a12e646e38b5cfb6c1f8`.

## Verification and limitations

- New positive regression failed before the implementation. Two positive policy
  scenarios, 32 rejection cases and retained-audit authority revocation pass.
- Cache coverage regression failed before the fix; added, edited, deleted and
  nested source-module checks now pass. Missing mandatory source fails closed.
- Final private diagnostic checked 35 residual photos across two days, made 40
  fresh original-code reads and explicitly REUSED retained body observations.
  Two additional correspondences passed; 33 swapped-photo bodies and 56 duplicate
  PDF-body counterexamples were rejected. 32.434 seconds; no external operations.
  This is not 89 new OCR reads, nor a complete-day replay or ground-truth accuracy.
- Both complete physical PDF pages were rendered with Poppler and visually
  checked against the original photos. Their visible numbers, business dates and
  document contents agree. These were validation checks, never planner inputs.
- A 33-photo / 606-crop wider larger-model diagnostic took 1612.749 seconds. It
  retained wrong prefixes and a known stable wrong suffix; it is NOT adopted as
  a blanket engine replacement. A separate 13-photo 2560-scale diagnostic took
  155.932 seconds and added zero independently confirmed codes; also not adopted.

- The complete Windows suite passed in the approved desktop user context:
  process/DPAPI, UI state, 235 ordinary tests (zero skipped), long-path OCR,
  pinned models, observation caches and four local-browser cases. The initial
  invocation was blocked by script policy; only the approved test process used
  an execution-policy override, without changing the system policy.
- A positioned integration invocation with a relative app path was rejected,
  as was the source repository without a staged layout runtime. Those are not
  passing integration runs. A properly staged candidate still needs that check.

Fresh full-day acceptance is tracked separately. The three previously recorded global-metric scene counterexamples
remain an explicit failing release gate; this code does not remove that gate.
Frozen annual, clean-PC and authorized online acceptance remain incomplete.
No release tag, deployment, main-branch merge, launcher change, date-default
change, login change or production business write is authorized by this report.
